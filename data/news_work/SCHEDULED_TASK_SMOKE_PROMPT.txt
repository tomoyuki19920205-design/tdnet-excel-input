```json
{

  "run_id": "<assignment_idと完全一致>",
  "ticker": "<assignmentのtickerと完全一致>",
  "checked_at": "<タイムゾーン付きISO-8601 datetime>",

```

enumは次の許可値からだけ選んでください。

- source_type: news | ir | company | government | exchange | research | other
- category: orders | backlog | pricing | demand | volume | product_mix | margin | raw_material | labor_cost | energy_cost | logistics | capacity | utilization | capex | new_factory | new_product | customer | supplier | competitor | industry | regulation | m_and_a | divestiture | reorganization | large_project | inventory | fx | shareholder_return | guidance | management_comment | other
- direction: positive | negative | mixed | neutral | unknown
- importance: high | medium | low
- earnings_relevance: direct | likely | general | context | unknown
- temporal_scope: current | fiscal_year | multi_year | ongoing | historical | unknown
- temporal_status: current | ongoing | historical | expired | unknown

checked_atとpublished_atは必ずタイムゾーン付きISO-8601にしてください。valid_fromとvalid_untilはnullまたはタイムゾーン付きISO-8601です。source_urlは実際に確認した原典または記事の絶対http/https URLにしてください。sources_checked_countは0以上の整数です。tagsは非空文字列だけのarrayです。記事全文、HTML、script、推測値は保存しないでください。
